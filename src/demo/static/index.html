Current Scientific Limitations & Operational Boundaries ▼
  • Regional & Temporal Window: Model validation is strictly bounded to the Central Bay of Bengal (Q1 2020). Multi-year, pan-Indian Ocean, and multi-season generalization is ongoing research.
  • Surface Regime Cold Bias: In the uppermost 0–20m layer, a systematic cold bias of -0.596 °C remains relative to in situ floats.
  • Deep Ocean Regimes (>200m): Performance in the deep ocean converges toward climatology without uniform variance reduction due to weak surface-depth thermal coupling.
  • GLORYS Reference Character: GLORYS12V1 is a numerical reanalysis model assimilating altimetry and SST, not absolute ground truth.
  • Operational Boundary: This system is an interactive Proof-of-Concept research prototype. It does NOT replace operational numerical ocean forecasting models and does NOT provide cyclone trajectory prediction.
